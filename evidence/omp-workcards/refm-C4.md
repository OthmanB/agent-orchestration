## Decisions (P)
- **D-1 Horizons**: block offset `b` after `fe`: 15 min = b 3, 30 min = b 6 (operative: served blocks 6…11 span 30–55 min), 60 min = b 12 (`diag_blocks`); all from the same call. Coverage and width are computed for every `b=0…12`; tables show the three.
- **D-2 Intervals**: central pairs of the 9 deciles: 10–90 (nominal 0.8), 20–80 (0.6), 30–70 (0.4), 40–60 (0.2), and 25–75 (0.5, `interp`) — nominal COVERAGE levels of the forecast intervals, unrelated to the p_raw boundaries 0.2/0.8. Per pair: coverage, miss-below, miss-above (nominal split ½ each); PIT histogram = count of the 9 quantiles below the observation → 10 bins (nominal 0.1 each). An asymmetric/heavy-tailed rx band is a correct answer when the miss split and PIT agree with the observed shape; reported with the empirical skew of the rx residual record.
- **D-3 Segments**: `pre_event` (blocks before the first anomaly onset: the model has never seen an anomaly) and `all_quiet` (`quiet_mask(margin_after=guard)`); FLAT/S1/real are identical in both. Cycles: all cycles from the shared first cycle (calibration needs no record; TimesFM has no ESS gate, so every cycle counts). Scenarios: FLAT, S1, S3, S4, S7 × seeds 1–3 × {uni, multi} at 2 d; context sweep (1 d, 1 h) seed 1 on FLAT, S1; real flat 10 pods × {uni, multi}. Signals in the tables: cpu, mem, rx_bytes, tx_bytes.
- **D-4 Width and honesty**: per signal and `b`: median band width (Q0.9−Q0.1) at raw-native and at block level, relative to the median observed level; compare (a) `w_stl` = Q0.9−Q0.1 of the STL-lite served record (the noise-law interval, `stl_pod(...).runs[n].record(k)`), reported for `stl-gate-off` (always available) and `stl-gate-on` (only where the as-deployed gate lets a model exist; S1 mostly not), (b) `w_rec` = the same on the TimesFM record (what the chain tolerates), (c) the TimesFM block band at b=6. Ratios `w_band(b=6)/w_stl` and `w_rec/w_stl` per signal and scenario; `band_wider` flag where >1. S3: band width after/before the noise-switch onset vs the STL record width ratio (does the band widen — regime awareness — or not).
- **D-5 Figures** (PNG ≥30 kB, height >800 px, `figures/`): `c4-bands-{S1,S3,S4}-{uni,multi}.png` via `build_figure(..., bands={short: served_band})` (served horizon, seed 1, 2 d; the p_raw strip is DB's three-class one); `c4-horizons-{S1,S3,S4}-multi.png` (3 signals × rows 15/30/60 min, one block per cycle, outer/inner band + median + observed block series); `c4-bands-realflat-{b4xkt,m7pr8}.png` (own Agg figure: per signal the last 2 d of block series with the served band, the raw-native 65-min fan for the first, middle and last model cycle, and a bottom p_raw strip (log-y) of the tfm row from `results/c3-real-flat.json` with horizontal lines at `scoring.alpha_threshold` (`decisive-normal boundary`) and `scoring.decisive_threshold` (`decisive-anomaly boundary`), the undecidable band shaded and the legend `decisively normal (<0.2)` / `undecidable [0.2, 0.8]` / `decisively anomalous (>0.8)`; thresholds read from the harness config, never retyped); `c4-width-vs-horizon.png` (relative width vs minutes ahead, real flat/S1/S3-pre, `w_stl` dashed); `c4-reliability.png` (nominal vs empirical coverage at 30 min per signal).
