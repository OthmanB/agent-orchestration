## The four quantities (exact; all on `p_raw`, strict `>`; `valid[k]=scored[k] ∧ k+1≥584`; anomaly events = `truth.anomaly_events()`; `guard=4`)
- **Detection** per anomaly event i: `search_end_i` = next anomaly onset (else n); `j*_i=min{k∈[onset_i,search_end_i): valid∧p_raw>thr}`; latency `j*−onset`; none ⇒ `censored`, `horizon=search_end−onset`.
- **FPR**: `quiet=truth.quiet_mask(margin_after=guard)`; `rate=#{valid∧quiet∧p_raw>thr}/#{valid∧quiet}` (+ n, count). Persistent events exclude onset→end of series; regimes (S3 noise switch) and gaps stay quiet.
- **Settling** per detected event: `start=end` (bounded) or `onset` (persistent); baseline = median `p_raw` of valid∧quiet ticks in the 288 ticks before onset (need ≥48, else `no_baseline`); `tol=max(0.05, 3·MAD)`; `s*=min{k≥max(start,j*): k+5<search_end, valid[k..k+5], p_raw[k..k+5]≤baseline+tol}`; `settle=s*−start`; none ⇒ `settle_censored`; undetected ⇒ `not_detected`.
- **Fidelity** per signal: `mask=valid_sig ∧ quiet ∧ ∀i(k<onset_i ∨ k≥end_i+582)` (`end=∞` if persistent); MAE, RMSE, bias of `observed−forecast` in native units, finite pairs only, `n`. Also fidelity per regime segment (split at regime onsets).
- Also stored per run: `p_raw` (NaN unscored), per-metric `p`, `strike=p<alpha_strike`, `observed`, `forecast`, `residual`; p_raw summary {median, q99, max, frac>thr, frac in flat band}.
