## 1. The blocker and the revised statistic (D1′)

RV reproduced through real ticks (n=144, π .01, τ .02, 0.2/0.8, floor 1): **A** `[.0013]+linspace(.0115,.0121,143)` ⇒ `x_(36)−x_(1)` = 1.034 → adopted, calm .0117 → 0.28; **B** `linspace(.0013,.01141,36)+linspace(.01142,.01202,108)` ⇒ 1.011 → adopted, calm → 0.49. Cause: `x_(k)−x_(1)` reads only the *first* k-window, so one low point or a broad bottom quartile certifies a narrow pile.

**Chosen: the narrowest k-window of the bulk ("shorth")** — `calm_span_ratio = min_{1≤i≤m−k+1} (x_(i+k−1) − x_(i)) / π`, on the sorted fit half, `m = calibration_order_index(n, τ)`, `k = max(1, ceil(m·normal/anomaly))` unchanged (36 at n=144). Window `i=1` is the old statistic, so **new ≤ old pointwise**: everything D1 rejected (live, sliding, slow-scenario piles) stays rejected. Meaning: the fitted bulk map rises by ≈ the normal threshold across any k bulk points, so this is the *steepest* 0.2-rise of the map, measured on the raw axis; guard ≥ floor ⇔ nowhere in the bulk may p_raw move by less than `floor·π` while the published value climbs a normal-threshold. In numpy: `float(np.min(srt[k-1:m] - srt[:m-k+1])) / prior` (same validation and `ValueError`s as today; `k=1` ⇒ 0 ⇒ reject).

Candidates on one table (ratio; floor 1.0; `/tmp/solo-p3-plan2/eval_stats.py`, gate passes on every adversarial row): C0 current `x_(k)−x_(1)` · C1 interior `x_(k)−x_(⌈k/2⌉)` · C2 IQR of lowest k · C3 fitted-map width for normal/2→normal · **C4 shorth**:
