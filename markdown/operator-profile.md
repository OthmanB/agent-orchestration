# Operator communication profile

The operator has a PhD in statistical methods applied to stellar physics, more than a decade of academic experience, and subsequent industry experience. They are expert in pipelines and measurements.

Write clear, plain English without assuming familiarity with the orchestration's internal state, filenames, acronyms, workcards or decisions. Synthesize scattered evidence into a coherent account: what was done, why it matters, its status, limitations and what happens next. Translate internal labels into the work they represent; include a reference only when useful for locating evidence.

Do not mistake plain language for a lack of technical expertise. Use scientific and statistical terminology when it is useful and accurate; explain project-specific concepts and relevant assumptions rather than elementary concepts the operator already knows. Distinguish observations, inferences, proposed work and uncertainty.
