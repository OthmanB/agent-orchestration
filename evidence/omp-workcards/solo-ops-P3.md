## Where P differs from R-P3 / S's pre-decisions (verified; D1-D3 are the escalations)

**D1 — guard statistic: R's `x_(m)−x_(1)` is refuted; use the lower-quartile ramp.** R's statistic is blind to contamination: 4 spikes (p_raw 0.98) in the 144-value fit half make it read 97.9π, the held-out gate passes on a clean second half, and the adopted map sends calm held-out ticks anywhere in [0.01, 0.79] (≈75 % above 0.2) — the original defect, never demoted. Live consequence (probe: slide each pod's real 288 `p_raw` forward with calm ticks bootstrapped from its own calm values, evaluate gate+guard per window): **R's statistic adopts a degenerate map on 10/10 pods 59-61 ticks (≈5 h) after rollout**, when the fleet-wide 2026-11-06 01:20-02:15 UTC burst (live history ticks 196-207, p_raw up to 0.99, 8-11 ticks >0.8 per pod) enters the fit half; iid-resampled live windows are adopted 25 % of the time.
